# TrynDraft User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Navigate to the Login page
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email, username, and password (min 8 characters)
4. Optionally add your League of Legends summoner name
5. Click "Create Account"

### Guest Mode
You can use TrynDraft without an account by clicking "Continue as Guest" on the login page. Guest users have full access to the draft tool but cannot save preferences or champion pools.

---

## Setting Up Your Profile

### Accessing Profile Settings
Click "Settings" in the navigation bar or the gear icon on the draft page.

### Setting Your Main Role
Click on one of the five role icons (Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC, Support) to select your main role. This will auto-populate when you enter the draft page.

### Setting Your Rank
Click on a rank icon to set your current rank. Recommendations will be tailored to your skill level.

### Choosing a Profile Picture
1. Click the search icon on your profile picture
2. Search for a champion
3. Click to select - the champion's splash art becomes your profile picture

### Building Your Champion Pool
1. Click "Add Champion" in the Champion Pool section
2. Search and select champions you play
3. Set proficiency levels (1-5 stars) for each champion
4. Champions in your pool will be prioritized in recommendations

## Using the Draft Tool

### Basic Controls
- **Side Selection**: Choose Blue or Red team
- **Role**: Your assigned role for this game
- **Rank**: ELO for matchup calculations
- **Phase Toggle**: Switch between BAN/PICK modes

### Banning Champions
1. Ensure you're in BAN phase (toggle shows "BAN")
2. Click on a ban slot to select it
3. Search for a champion in the picker
4. Click to ban - the champion will appear in the slot

### Picking Champions
1. Switch to PICK phase using the toggle
2. Click on a pick slot to select it
3. Search and click a champion to pick
4. Champions are automatically removed from the available pool

### Drag and Drop
- Drag champions between slots to rearrange
- Drag a champion back to the picker to remove it
- Drag from the picker to a slot to assign

### Resetting the Draft
Click "Reset" to clear all picks and bans. Your role and rank settings will be preserved.

## Understanding Recommendations

### Top Recommendation
The AI's best champion suggestion for your current situation, considering:
- Your champion pool
- Team composition
- Enemy matchups
- Meta statistics

### Draft Advantage Meter
Shows which team has the advantage based on:
- Champion synergies
- Counter matchups
- Team composition balance

### LLM Analysis
Strategic insights including:
- Current draft assessment
- Win conditions for both teams
- Suggested gameplans
- Composition strengths/weaknesses

---

## Tips for Better Drafts

1. **Set up your champion pool** - Recommendations are much better when the AI knows what you can play
2. **Consider team composition** - Don't pick 5 AD champions
3. **Watch the advantage meter** - If it's swinging against you, prioritize counter-picks
4. **Read the LLM analysis** - It often catches synergies and counters you might miss
